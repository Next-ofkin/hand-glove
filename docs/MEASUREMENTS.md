# Measure both hands

Updated: **7 November 2026 (Africa/Lagos)**  
Project scope: **one LEFT glove and one RIGHT glove**  
Status: **no measurements recorded yet**

Use a ruler or soft measuring tape and record centimetres. Measure each hand separately with fingers comfortably straight, following the back of the finger. Do not estimate the second hand from the first.

## Start here: index and thumb samples

| Measurement | LEFT (cm) | RIGHT (cm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Index: main knuckle at palm (MCP) to middle joint (PIP) | Pending | Pending |
| Index: middle joint (PIP) to joint nearest nail (DIP) | Pending | Pending |
| Index: joint nearest nail (DIP) to fingertip | Pending | Pending |
| Thumb: main thumb knuckle near palm (MCP) to bending joint (IP) | Pending | Pending |
| Thumb: bending joint (IP) to tip | Pending | Pending |

The thumb MCP is the visible thumb knuckle nearer the palm; it is not the thumb-base CMC region near the wrist. Thumb-base sensing needs a separate placement trial through opposition. These five measurements per hand start the fit assessment; they do not by themselves determine a validated sensor part number.

Mark LEFT/RIGHT paper templates with the joint centres and intended sensor termination routes. Recheck the route when comfortably bent and during thumb opposition. Ask suppliers to match active sensing-zone spacing, allowable bend radius and termination geometry; total strip length alone does not establish fit.

Each hand will also need its own sensor ranges, neutral pose, orientation alignment and calibration record. Do not mirror numerical calibration values or assume the same axes without testing.
